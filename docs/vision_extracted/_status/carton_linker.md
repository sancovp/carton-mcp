## THE LINKER NEVER SPLITS A TOKEN AT A CLOSING PARENTHESIS
| # | requirement | cases followed | where each is decided (path:line) | what happens to each case | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | A token ending in a closing parenthesis is linked whole or not at all: `doc(v)` stays `doc(v)`, and no link is cut through the middle of a token. | the literal token `doc(v)` appears in a description; the Aho-Corasick automaton scans over it and `before_ok/after_ok` look at the chars around the matched span | add_concept_tool.py:830-833 — `before_ok = start_idx == 0 or not desc_lower[start_idx - 1].isalnum()`; `after_ok = end_idx + 1 >= len(desc_lower) or not desc_lower[end_idx + 1].isalnum()`. `isalnum` is False for `)`, so the closing paren is treated as a boundary, not as part of the token: when the automaton's matched span ends at the `)`, `after_ok` is True (the `)` is non-alnum, so the post-match check passes), and the whole span including the `)` is replaced wholesale by the link `[doc(v)](...doc(v)/doc(v)_itself.md)` | `doc(v)` is matched as a single token bounded by the `)` at its end; the matcher takes or skips the whole token, never the part inside the parentheses. The `)` is NOT part of the matched text (it is a post-match boundary char), so the replacement at :865-866 (which uses `description[start_idx:end_idx]` — the matcher-reported indices) wraps the literal text including the `)` and never cuts through it | REMAINING (verifier; mapper said BUILT) |
